## RML-01 — 1901–1903: Post-Panic Multiple Compression

**CAPE at peak:** ~21x (elevated for era)
**Monetary stance:** Tightening (gold standard constraint)
**Trigger:** Railroad trust speculation + J.P. Morgan Northern Securities antitrust action
**Chain:** Trust-busting uncertainty → capital withdrawal from speculative railroads →
  equity multiple compression → Equities → SHORT
**Drawdown:** ~46% peak-to-trough
**Recovery:** ~4 years to new high
**M-score anchor:** Regulatory regime change as a stand-alone multiple compression trigger
  independent of earnings deterioration.

## RML-02 — 1907: Knickerbocker Panic (Pre-Fed Leverage Cascade)

**CAPE at peak:** ~14x
**Monetary stance:** No central bank backstop
**Trigger:** Copper speculation collapse → trust company run
**Chain:** Leverage cascade (no lender of last resort) → liquidity seizure →
  forced asset liquidation → Equities, Credit → TACTICAL
**Drawdown:** ~49%
**Recovery:** ~3 years
**M-score anchor:** Absence of central bank backstop converts moderate leverage into
  systemic cascade. Recovery duration = function of private-sector balance sheet repair,
  not policy response.

## RML-03 — 1921: Secular Bull Entry (Depression Trough)

**CAPE at entry:** ~5x (maximum depression)
**Monetary stance:** Easing (Fed cutting after 1920–21 deflation)
**Trigger:** Easing at extreme CAPE → multiple expansion from depressed base
**Chain:** CAPE 5x + Fed easing → discount rate compression → multiple expansion →
  Equities → SECULAR
**Subsequent return:** ~500% over 8 years (1921–1929)
**M-score anchor:** CAPE below 7x + active easing = highest-probability secular bull
  entry signal in the full 125-year record.

## RML-04 — 1929: Great Crash (Leverage-Cascade + CAPE Extreme)

**CAPE at peak:** ~32x
**Monetary stance:** Tightening (Fed raised rates 1928–29)
**Trigger:** Margin debt at record (broker loans ~$8.5B) + Fed tightening
**Chain:** CAPE 32x + tightening → margin call cascade → forced deleveraging →
  no Fed backstop → banking system failure → Equities (−89%), Credit, Economy → SECULAR
**Drawdown:** 89% peak-to-trough (1929–1932)
**Recovery:** 25 years to real new high
**M-score anchor:** CAPE extreme + tightening + leverage extreme = maximum fragility
  configuration. Recovery duration determined by leverage unwind depth, not policy
  intent (Smoot-Hawley amplified, not caused).

## RML-05 — 1949: Secular Bull Entry (Post-War Trough)

**CAPE at entry:** ~10x
**Monetary stance:** Neutral-to-easing (Fed-Treasury Accord 1951 preceded by peg)
**Trigger:** Post-war reconversion complete; corporate earnings normalizing
**Chain:** CAPE 10x + earnings normalization + pent-up consumer demand →
  multiple expansion → Equities → SECULAR
**Subsequent return:** ~600% over 17 years (1949–1966)
**M-score anchor:** CAPE below 12x + stable/easing policy + earnings recovery =
  secular bull entry. Validates CAPE 7–12x as the highest-return entry zone.

## RML-06 — 1966–1982: Secular Bear (Inflation Regime + CAPE Compression)

**CAPE at 1966 peak:** ~24x
**Monetary stance:** Progressively tightening (Volcker 1979)
**Trigger:** Vietnam fiscal expansion + oil shocks → inflation regime shift
**Chain:** Inflation → real earnings erosion → CAPE compression from 24x to 7x →
  Equities (flat nominal, −50% real) → SECULAR
**Duration:** 16 years
**Real return:** Negative
**M-score anchor:** Inflation regime shift is a CAPE compression mechanism independent
  of nominal earnings growth. Real multiple compression can occur with rising nominal prices.

## RML-07 — 1982: Secular Bull Entry (Volcker Pivot)

**CAPE at entry:** ~7x
**Monetary stance:** Pivoting to easing (Fed funds rate cut from 20% peak)
**Trigger:** Volcker disinflation complete; CAPE at 60-year low
**Chain:** CAPE 7x + aggressive easing + disinflation → discount rate compression →
  maximum multiple expansion → Equities → SECULAR
**Subsequent return:** ~1,700% over 18 years (1982–2000)
**M-score anchor:** Maximum secular bull return requires CAPE near historical floor +
  aggressive easing entry. The 1982 entry is the cleanest textbook case in the record.

## RML-08 — 1987: Black Monday (Microstructure Cascade)

**CAPE at peak:** ~20x
**Monetary stance:** Tightening (Fed raised rates through 1987)
**Trigger:** Portfolio insurance feedback loop + program trading
**Chain:** Portfolio insurance sell triggers → price decline → more insurance triggers →
  endogenous cascade (not fundamental-driven) → Fed backstop (Greenspan put introduced) →
  Equities (−23% single day) → TACTICAL
**Recovery:** 2 years to new high
**M-score anchor:** Microstructure fragility (portfolio insurance, 1987; HFT, 2010;
  0DTE options, present) creates endogenous crash risk independent of valuation.
  Fed backstop = regime change → recovery in months vs. years.

## RML-09 — 1998: LTCM / Russia (Liquidity Seizure, Contained)

**CAPE at event:** ~40x
**Monetary stance:** Easing (Fed cut 3x in emergency)
**Trigger:** Russia default → LTCM leverage unwind → liquidity seizure
**Chain:** Leverage extreme + correlated book → forced unwind → Fed emergency easing →
  contained cascade → Equities (−20% brief), Credit → TACTICAL
**Recovery:** Months (Fed backstop operational)
**M-score anchor:** Even at CAPE 40x, Fed emergency easing can contain a liquidity
  event and extend the cycle. Does NOT reset valuation risk — it defers it.
  CAPE 40x + contained crisis = closest historical analogue to current configuration.

## RML-10 — 2000–2002: Dot-Com Secular Bear (CAPE Extreme + Earnings Collapse)

**CAPE at peak:** ~44x (all-time record)
**Monetary stance:** Tightening (Fed raised rates 1999–2000)
**Trigger:** Earnings normalization from tech bubble + Fed tightening
**Chain:** CAPE 44x + tightening → multiple compression from extreme →
  tech earnings collapse → Equities (−50%), Nasdaq (−78%) → SECULAR
**Duration:** 2000–2012 (real return flat over 12 years)
**M-score anchor:** CAPE extreme + tightening is the highest-probability secular bear
  trigger. At CAPE >40x, even modest tightening or earnings deceleration is sufficient
  to initiate secular compression. Current CAPE 39.8x is directly in this zone.

## RML-11 — 2008–2009: GFC (Leverage Cascade + Credit Seizure)

**CAPE at peak:** ~27x
**Monetary stance:** Late tightening → emergency easing
**Trigger:** Subprime mortgage leverage unwind → bank balance sheet impairment
**Chain:** Housing leverage extreme → ABX collapse → interbank credit seizure →
  MinMaSS breach (market-maker withdrawal) → Equities (−57%), Credit, Economy → STRUCTURAL
**Recovery:** 4 years to nominal new high (2013); 5 years real
**M-score anchor:** MinMaSS (Minimum Market Size for Stability) breach occurs when
  dealer balance sheets contract simultaneously. This is distinct from a valuation
  correction — it is a plumbing failure requiring balance sheet repair, not rate cuts.

## RML-12 — 2020: COVID Crash (Exogenous Shock + Fed Backstop)

**CAPE at pre-crash:** ~31x
**Monetary stance:** Emergency easing (Fed unlimited QE within days)
**Trigger:** Pandemic-induced economic shutdown
**Chain:** Exogenous shock → earnings collapse → Fed unlimited backstop →
  liquidity restored → multiple re-expansion to new CAPE extreme →
  Equities (−34% then +100%) → TACTICAL
**Recovery:** 5 months to new high (fastest in record)
**M-score anchor:** Exogenous-shock crashes with intact Fed backstop recover in
  months. The Fed’s 2020 response did not reset valuation risk — it elevated CAPE
  from 31x to 39x, creating the current late-cycle configuration.
